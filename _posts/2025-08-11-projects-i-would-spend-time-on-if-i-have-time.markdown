## Managing multiple directions

Something that's always been hard for me is having ideas come into my brain all the time for things to do. I never seem to have time to finish everything I want to and am always juggling multiple projects along with work and life.

### AI-Powered Cat Toy

The latest Raspberry Pi has an integrated graphics chip which has opened up a lot of possibilities for tinkering. For example you can do computer vision tasks processing image/video data locally. What I've been testing the is the idea of simulating prey behavior, coming up with a controller capable of activating the prey drive of cats. Cat toys on the market are notoriously expensive compared to the interest they generate from cats. My goal has been to create a device capable of consistently entertaining and learning about the behavior of the cat I have at home. So far I've mounted a camera and distance sensor on an RC Car chasis, I think there is a lot of potential to collect data and train a model that 1. can chase the cat around 2. get the cat chasing it around. Forget Human Computer Interaction, we want Cat Computer Interaction.

However, this does represent a lot of work and time and I need to clear one of my active projects from my plate to give this the attention it deserves.
