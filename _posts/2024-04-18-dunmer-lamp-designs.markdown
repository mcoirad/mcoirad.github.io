## Making my first lamp

This past year I've come to have access to a hackerspace, something that I've never utilized before and honestly I feel like I've really missed out!

One of the first things I've tried to do is get the hang of the laser cutters.

My first project was to make a lamp, which I did through a class at the hackerspace. This is a super basic project that requires no IRL fabrication skills so seemed like a good start.

My 2D design was centered around making a rectangular shape but avoiding the visible finger joints common in a lot of laser cut pieces. My solution was a bit ambitious as it led to a very difficult assembly process.

![First lamp 2D design](/assets/images/lamps/lamp1.png)


The many small parts in the design made it a bit difficult to punch out all the parts that had been cut out. The laser cutter also didn't cut through everything, even though I made 2 passes with the laser. Curved pieces in particular didn't do the best.

However, over a few days I was eventually able to glue it together!

![Assembling the lamp](/assets/images/lamps/lamp2.jpg)
![Assembling the lamp](/assets/images/lamps/lamp3.jpg)
![Lamp is assembled!](/assets/images/lamps/lamp4.jpg)
