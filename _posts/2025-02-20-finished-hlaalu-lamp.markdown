## Finished a lamp

Not much but I finally finished staining and putting together a lamp I had designed for a gift based on House Hlaalu from Morrowind.

House Hlaalu being the most mercantile and pro-Empire of the three Morrowind houses.
![Hlaalu Castle](/assets/images/lamps/hlaalucastle.jpg)
![Hlaalu Meme](/assets/images/lamps/hlaalumeme.png)

I think I joined the Telvanni in my playthrough but I was always an Imperial sympathizer. Nobody likes the Camonna Tong!

[I found this video to be helpful for an intro to staining the wood.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8r7xmuSx6Pg)

### Pretty happy with the final product
![Morrowind Hlaalu Lamp](/assets/images/lamps/finished_hlaalu1.jpg)

![Morrowind Hlaalu Lamp](/assets/images/lamps/finished_hlaalu2.jpg)
